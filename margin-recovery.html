What we build

One system. Seven parts. Your code, your data.

01

COD risk scoring

Every COD order scored by pin code, customer history, order value and time of day before it ships.

02

WhatsApp confirm + prepaid nudge

Risky orders confirmed on WhatsApp. No reply, no dispatch. A UPI link with a small discount goes out within minutes.

03

Voice calls in Hindi and English

An AI caller verifies high-risk COD orders and follows up failed deliveries. Every call outcome is logged.

04

Failed-delivery recovery

Reschedules, fixes addresses and chases the courier the same day, instead of waiting for the return.

05

Courier routing by pin code

Each order goes to the courier that actually delivers in that pin code, based on your own delivery history.

06

Payout matching

Shopify, Razorpay, COD remittances, Amazon and Flipkart settlements matched to orders. Short payments flagged.

07

True-margin dashboard on your phone

Margin after returns, fees and ad spend, by channel, SKU, pin code and courier. Refreshed daily, read-only.

Questions

Before you book

We already use GoKwik or Shopflo. Why do we need this?

Keep them. They work at checkout. This system covers what happens after the order: confirmation, failed deliveries, courier choice, payout matching and true margin. Your audit shows how much still leaks with your current tools in place.

Do we have to replace any of our tools?

No. We connect to Shopify, your courier aggregator, your WhatsApp provider and your marketplaces. If an app is costing more than it saves, the audit will say so, and the choice is yours.

Who owns the system?

You do. Code, data and accounts are in your company's name on final payment. No per-user or per-order fee to us.

What do you need from us for the audit?

Read-only exports for the last 6 months: Shopify orders, courier shipment and NDR reports, COD remittance reports, payment gateway and marketplace settlements. We sign an NDA before you share anything.

How fast will we see results?

COD confirmation and failed-delivery recovery usually go live first, within the first half of the build. The guarantee is measured over the 60 days after launch.

What about customer data and calls?

We only use the order and contact data you already hold, for order verification and delivery follow-up. Consent and calling rules are agreed with your team in the contract.
